Happy Birthday My Lilly 🌸

It's been almost 7 years now since I saw a pretty soul in my school ally whom I can't seem to leave forever. You barged into my heart like a comet and now you have your name etched deep inside it. You travelled with me through the peaks and the valleys of my life. How can I ever fully thank you? It's stupid to shine a light towards the moon. You are the moon of my life. The light that brightened up the darkest days of my life. All these people in the world, and there's not a single soul that can even come close to you. How can a person be so perfect in all the ways a person can be? How can a person be really good at everything she puts herself in?

Sometimes I think of our destiny — of how blessed I am to have you in my life. And sometimes I think of how worse my life would have been if I didn't have you. My soul suffocates at the slight thought of it. Isn't it crazy that I was blessed to see you grow through these years. At every phase of my life since high-school, I would think to myself, ‘You are the closest person to my soul’, and then you would prove me wrong by getting even closer in the next phase. I didn't know how deeply two souls could connect until I was with you. I can't live without you. My life depends on you more than anyone else. No. It doesn't depend on anyone else, just you.

Please take care of yourself, dear. I know life has been harsh on you, You don't deserve all the pain and toxicity you are dealing with. Allah likes to test his dearest servants. He will always make you feel that you're not meant to be here, you are meant to be in Jannah. And one day, when you finally reach your home, you will get to know why you had to feel nothing here.

Don't worry too much. You will be alright. As you always do. I will always look up to you, the way you endure everything with your quiet strength. I'll always pray to lighten your test too. And I promise to make sure to ease your life however I can. Thank you for staying with me, eventhough I hurted you a lot. Thanking you for forgiving me again and again. I'll always be there with you, for the rest of my life. In Sha Allah. And I'll pray for you to have a much calmer, peaceful and more tranquil year.

With Love,
Insaf
